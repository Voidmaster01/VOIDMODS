# VOIDMODS 💜
Mods for **FNAF Online Multiplayer**, made by Voidmaster01 (MelonLoader 0.7)

## 🧩 The mods
**ModConfigs**: a **MOD CONFIGS** row on the home screen, one in-game place for the settings of all your mods.
**Voidcons**: your own **round HD player icon** on the character select screen. Pick any picture (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF...), move and crop it right in the game. *Needs ModConfigs.*

## 📥 Install in one click
1. Have **MelonLoader 0.7** in your game folder (start the game once after installing it)
2. Close the game
3. Grab an installer from **Releases** and double-click it:
 • `VOIDMODS-FULL.bat`: everything
 • `VOIDMODS-Voidcons.bat`: Voidcons + ModConfigs
 • `VOIDMODS-ModConfigs.bat`: just the hub
It finds the game by itself and backs up anything it replaces.
**FULL is always the release marked "Latest".** Only want or need one mod? Look through the Releases list for its own installer.

## ℹ️ Good to know
• Icons travel through the lobby **host**, so the host and everyone who wants to see icons need Voidcons
• Backers keep their backer icon unless *they* switch it in MOD CONFIGS → Voidcons
• Voidcons adds no privacy risk: it never sends, logs or saves player IDs (the game itself already shares them in its player list)
